Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn Tallyz?

Tallyz can be earned by creating popular games, selling clothes in the Marketplace, or through daily login bonuses for members!

Is BLYPE free to play?

Yes! BLYPE is a free online building engine. While you can buy Tallyz for special items, building and playing is 100% free.

Safety & Privacy

We take safety seriously. All chat is filtered, and we have a dedicated team of moderators. Check the Parents' Corner for more details.
