Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get more Blypo-Bux (B$)?
You can earn B$ by playing games, participating in community events, or receiving them from an Administrator.
I forgot my password! What do I do?
Since BLYPEME is currently in development, please contact a Staff member in the Forum to have your password reset manually.
How do I wear the items I bought?
Go to the 'Character' tab in the navigation menu. There you can see your inventory and click 'Wear' on any item you own.
